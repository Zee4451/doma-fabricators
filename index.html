SAFETY WITHOUT COMPROMISE

At Doma Fabricators, safety is our top priority. We maintain comprehensive safety management systems designed to prevent accidents, protect the environment, and ensure compliance with all relevant safety regulations.

01

— Risk Assessment & PPE Compliance

Identifying and controlling hazards before work begins. Providing and enforcing the use of personal protective equipment at all times.

02

— Training, Audits & Inspections

Regular safety training, drills, and workshops for all employees. Routine checks to maintain strict safety standards across all operations.

03

— Emergency Preparedness & Compliance

Well-defined response plans, regular mock drills, full adherence to all safety laws, and continuous improvement aligned with best practices.
